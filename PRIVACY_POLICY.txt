Privacy Policy

Warning.AI is built and maintained as a free app. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users about the policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone who uses this app.

Information Collection and Use

Warning.AI does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, or registration of any kind.

Camera

The app requests access to your device camera solely to perform real-time on-device scene analysis. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device and are never saved, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

Internet Access

The app requires a one-time internet connection on first launch to download the AI model files (~30 MB total) from Google's public servers. After this initial download, the app works fully offline. No user data is sent during or after this download.

On-Device Processing

All AI inference (object detection and scene classification) runs entirely on your device using TensorFlow Lite. No images, video, or analysis results are ever sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service solely for the one-time model download:

Google APIs / Cloud Storage

No analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or tracking libraries are included in this app.

Children's Privacy

This app does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
